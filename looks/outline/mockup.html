The Autumn Retreat

An outline you open one toggle at a time. Text is Bellefair, this title is Tangerine, and the small capitals are Josefin Sans. Every line sits at double spacing.

Before we gather

Invitations

Send the first notice six weeks ahead, with the date, the place and who to ask about rides.

What the card says

Saturday the eighth of November, ten in the morning until four.

Bring a warm layer, a journal and something to share at lunch.

Who sends reminders

The hospitality team, two weeks out and again the Thursday before.

The room
  • Chairs in a wide circle, with space between them to walk
  • A low table in the centre for candles and stones
  • Tea station by the windows, not the door

The day itself

Morning

Arrive in quiet. A welcome, a short reading, then twenty minutes of silence before anyone speaks.

The opening reading

Choose something under two minutes, read slowly, and leave a full breath before the silence begins.

Afternoon

Small circles of four. Each person speaks once without being answered, then the circle opens for conversation.

Closing

Everyone names one thing they are taking home. The candles go out together.
