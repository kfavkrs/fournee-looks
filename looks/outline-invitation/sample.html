You are warmly invited to

The Autumn Retreat

A quiet day of reflection, conversation and good bread, among friends old and new.

The particulars

When

Saturday the eighth of November, from ten in the morning until four in the afternoon.

Where

The garden room, upstairs from the main hall.

Finding your way

Come in by the side door on the courtyard. The stairs are just past the coat rack, and the lift is beside them.

What to bring
  • A warm layer for the morning silence
  • A journal or a few loose pages
  • Something to share at lunch

The day itself

Morning

Arrive in quiet. A welcome, a short reading, then twenty minutes of silence before anyone speaks.

Afternoon

Small circles of four, then an unhurried walk in the garden.

Closing

Everyone names one thing they are taking home. The candles go out together.

Kindly reply by the first of November

to the hospitality team, so we can set a place for you.
